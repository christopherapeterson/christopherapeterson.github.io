Project Overview

Despite managing nearly $50M in annual ad spend, Algofy wasn’t using its website to attract new clients or showcase its expertise. I led a full redesign of the site to position it as a client acquisition tool, highlighting their authority in performance marketing and supporting their growth goals.

Final Outcome

The new site is clean, strategic, and conversion-focused, reflecting the caliber of work Algofy delivers. It now serves as a proper front door to the brand, helping attract leads, communicate services clearly, and elevate their online presence.

In addition to their main site, I also helped them build a separate site for their sister company Algofy Outdoors, built to help them achieve higher organic rankings within the outdoor niche and showcase their brand as a true industry leader.
